THE MINING PROCESS

Start with a decision,
not a pile of data.

01Define the goal

What decision or outcome are we trying to improve?

02Prepare the data

03Choose a method

QUICK REFERENCE

Glossary for
the next question.

Keep this nearby as you explore more advanced courses, tools, and projects.

01What is ETL?+

ETL stands for extract, transform, load. It is the pipeline that takes data out of source systems, cleans and reshapes it, and loads it into a warehouse.

02Is a data warehouse the same as a database?+

Both store data, but they are optimized for different jobs. A transactional database supports fast, current operations; a warehouse is designed to combine history and answer analytical questions.

03Does data mining always use AI?+

No. Data mining can use statistics, rules, clustering, or machine learning. “AI” is one family of techniques, not a requirement for every pattern-finding task.

04What makes a pattern useful?+

A useful pattern is reliable enough to repeat, meaningful in context, and connected to an action or decision. A surprising correlation is only a starting clue.
